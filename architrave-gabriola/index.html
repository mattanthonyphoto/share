The fee gets lighter. The shoot does not.

The architect, the interior designer, and the trades whose work shows up in the pictures can share the photography fee. Millwork, windows, glazing. Not the homeowner.

The share is on the $2,000 photography fee only. A 30% uplift is added for each vendor who joins, and that new total is split evenly. Travel days and travel costs stay with Architrave.
