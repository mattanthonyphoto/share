April

Setting the foundation

The first month was about learning your voice properly and building the system everything else would run on.

  • Creative workshop at your studio, April 13. Eight voice recordings captured and transcribed into a working voice guide, so the writing sounds like you rather than like an agency.
  • Your client portal opened, with shared folders for deliverables and a review queue where you approve or send back any post.
  • Your whole body of work catalogued. 17 projects and 597 images pulled in, so content gets built from your actual projects.
  • Four content pillars set, with sample captions and seasonal themes, so the feed has a shape instead of being one off posts.
  • Five project content packages produced: Sunstone, Balsam, SRAM, Sunridge and Tapleys. Each one carrying carousels, single posts, story sequences, Pinterest pins and LinkedIn posts.
  • 21 story videos produced and set to music.
  • Content calendar opened for your approval on April 20, and the first posts went live the same day.
  • Conibear photographed on April 30 at First Tracks in Whistler Creekside.
  • Editorial calendar guide delivered, covering how the posting rhythm works across the year.
02

May

The website, rebuilt around your words

The first draft went out on May 5. Your notes on May 11 sent it back to the drawing board, and that was the right call. The rebuild pulled your existing writing through instead of inventing new copy.

  • First version delivered May 5, 47 pages.
  • Rebuilt to 32 pages after your feedback, with the copy taken word for word from your live site wherever you had already said it better.
  • Review call May 12, first revision round built and deployed May 14.
  • Your 18 page feedback document applied in full and deployed May 25. Navigation and footer rewritten, services restructured into your five phases, press list corrected, projects split into residential and commercial.
  • Team section built with bios and portraits for all five of you.
  • Careers, testimonials and contact pages rewritten in your language, including the enquiry process and the phrasing you asked for.
  • Posting carried on throughout, with carousels, stories, Pinterest pins and Google Business posts going out on schedule.
03

June

Live, and the search layer underneath

The site went live, and the work moved to what sits under it. The parts nobody sees, but search engines read.

  • Your new website went live on June 2.
  • Mobile pass across every page, all banner images moved onto your own image library, and the contact form wired so enquiries land in your inbox.
  • Search and AI visibility layer built. Alt text written for 699 images, structured data deployed across the site, page titles and descriptions written for all 33 pages and handed to you as a working sheet, sitemap and internal links set, and access opened for the AI engines so your studio can be named in ChatGPT and similar answers.
  • Two full reports delivered June 3, one on the website and search work, one on the social strategy.
  • Conibear photographs delivered June 8, then regraded to a warmer edit after your notes.
  • 15 project reels built, then redesigned after your June 10 notes into four distinct formats so no two read the same, and retimed to your music.
  • Sitemap guide delivered, showing every page and where it sits.
04

July

Reels, refined to your ear

July went into the reels, and into getting the cutting right rather than close enough.

  • Reels rebuilt to follow the rhythm of the track rather than a fixed shot length, using your notes and the two reference reels you sent.
  • SRAM approved as the direction, then all seven remaining project reels rebuilt to match it.
  • 21 image variations produced, three per project, so you can pick the edit that reads best for each home rather than taking the only version.
  • Sourcing Trips montage built from your behind the scenes folder, in three music versions for you to choose between.
  • Conibear redelivered July 15: 39 images in both web and full resolution in the moody edit, plus the reel.
  • Two trackers shared with you, one for music and reels, one for voice memos, so you can see at a glance which track sits on which reel and which projects still need your notes.
  • Client portal instructions delivered July 2, with the updated workflow.
  • Pinterest scheduled through to August 22.
